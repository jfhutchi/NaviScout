Know the direction

Camera-up guidance, a world-north indicator, signed height and age labels, plus an edge arrow for the nearest missing target beyond your range.

Keep your progress

Unobtained supplies remain locked. Refills follow your current bags, wallet, hearts and magic meter.

Choose your assists

Refill once or continuously. Minigame ammunition and timer hold have separate controls.

What you can turn on

  • Collectible guidance: Golden Skulltulas and heart-piece locations, excluding collected items.
  • Alerts: separate skull/heart filters, distance range and optional sound.
  • Supplies: rupees, sticks, seeds, nuts, bombs, Bombchus, arrows, Magic Beans and Scrub seeds.
  • Existing meters: refill current heart containers and an obtained magic meter.
  • Link: independent meter refills, 2x run/swim speed, separate 2x roll speed, 2x jump height, 2x enemy damage, enemy stealth and Off / 2x / Unlimited dive depth.
  • Area progress: collected/total Skulltulas and hearts, filtered by Child, Adult, shared or unspecified availability.
  • Minigames: rented ammunition and optional timer hold for the supported timed activities.

Read this before installing

This is a separate window, not a patch to the game's original map. It does not grant equipment, bags, spells, rewards, bottle contents or quest items.

Game files and saves are not included. Your radar catalog is generated locally from your own game. Your existing profile is backed up during first-run setup.

New refill, movement, damage, stealth, dive and minigame assists start off. Enable only the assists you want.

Other builds, Hookshot, Lichii and emulators are outside this release's compatibility.

Four separate control tabs

Your progress, area by area.

The new Progress tab shows collected/total Skulltulas and heart locations for each area. All, Child, Adult and Unknown filters describe location availability. Shared locations appear in both age filters and only once in All.

All 36 heart locations and 53 overworld Skulltulas have age metadata from the game maps. The remaining 47 dungeon Skulltulas are unspecified. The save does not record the age of past pickups; equipment, night-time and quest requirements still apply.

Version 1.2 also fixes chest-based hearts using the wrong collection condition. Existing catalogs regenerate locally, and previously collected chest rewards disappear correctly.

Outside the circle? Follow the arrow.

Gold means Skulltula; pink means heart piece. Nearest-target and radar labels show signed height (+ up, - down) and Child/Adult availability. Height points to the mapped location, which can be an entrance or reward anchor. The arrow rotates with the camera and becomes a normal marker inside range.

Install separately

  1. Extract the full release ZIP. Keep the guide and screenshots together.
  2. Run NaviScout-Setup-1.2.0.exe. Review the installer, then select Install Navi Scout.
  3. Start Navi Scout. Close NX2navi once and browse to your own ocarina.exe.
  4. Select Prepare my game. A verified profile backup and your private radar catalog are created.
  5. Play normally. Press F8 to hide/show. Hiding keeps enabled assists running; closing stops them.

No administrator access or separate Python install is needed.

Installing Navi Scout does not move your game. Application files live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\NaviScout; personal settings and backups live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\NaviScout.

Using the controls

Items / Keep topped up refills checked supplies roughly four times per second. Refill once fills them once. Items unlock only when obtained; caps follow upgrades.

Link has independent meter-refill buttons. Run / swim speed 2x doubles normal running and swimming, including swim dash and heavy variants. Roll speed 2x and Jump height 2x are separate switches. All restore on switching off. The brown/gold menu styling and On/Off switches follow the in-game settings menu. Dive depth remains independent. Jump height affects normal jumps; it adds no jump key.

Damage 2x independently boosts positive processed damage from Link-owned attacks against current enemies. It uses the shared hit-processing stage for melee and owned projectiles. Incoming damage to Link is unchanged; immunity, forced-one-damage rules and scripted caps remain authoritative. Native-helper tests and code traces pass, but live combat and individual weapon coverage are still pending.

Invisible to enemies blocks new perception-based detection while keeping Link visible to you. Turn it on before approaching enemies. Existing aggro, scripted fights and bosses may still attack; friendly NPC awareness may also change. Off removes only Navi Scout's own reason. A paused game may need to resume briefly for application or cleanup.

Dive limit offers Off, 2x scale depth, or Unlimited depth. Your scale is not upgraded. This extends depth-limited dives; it is not an oxygen-timer or universal drowning-immunity cheat. Normal swimming and collision rules still apply.

Minigames / Keep round ammo topped up uses that tab's independent Bombchu, seed and arrow choices and the round's original allowance.

Freeze minigame timer holds current time for diving, Dampe's race, horse race, cucco search or running race. It is independent of automatic supplies. Uncheck to resume.

Practical limits

Markers follow area boundaries and may require night, equipment, progression or a different floor. Health refills are not invulnerability; zero health is not resurrected. Timer holds can show tiny subsecond movement between updates.

Build checks and layout checks reject unsupported games. Timed trading quests are excluded from timer hold. Other games' internal timing may not use the supported shared timer.

132 automated tests pass. The player confirmed running, swimming, rolling, jumping and both dive modes. Enemy stealth On and Off were confirmed in a live enemy encounter on NX2navi 0.9.2; original-code restoration and cleanup were also verified. Run/swim, damage and enemy stealth require the tested 0.9.2 Windows code signatures and reject unrecognized or rebuilt executables. Movement, damage and dive restoration runs on Off or close; restart the game if the helper is forcibly terminated or reports a restore failure.

Bombchu bowling and health refills were checked live. The player confirmed diving timer hold. Not every minigame mode has been tested in a live round or on a second PC.

Review, remove, or restore

This community build is unsigned. Review its source, license and SHA-256 checksum list. No security setting needs to be disabled. The application has no telemetry, service, scheduled task or background network listener.

Remove it through Windows Installed apps or its Start menu uninstaller. Your game, settings and backups remain. To restore a profile, close the game/helper, preserve the current profile, and copy back the backup's complete profile folder, including its save journal.
